title: "Temple and Worship"
order: 2
summary: "Where and how God is worshipped: tabernacle, temple and synagogue, then the Lord's Supper, church buildings, icons and the liturgies of East and West."
---

This thread follows the place of worship from the tent in the wilderness to Solomon's temple, its destruction and rebuilding, and its final loss in AD 70. It then follows Christian worship: the breaking of bread described by Paul and Justin, the great churches built from the fourth century, the disputes over icons, and the reforms of worship in the sixteenth and twentieth centuries.
