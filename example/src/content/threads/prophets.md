---
title: "Prophets and Prophecy"
order: 4
summary: "Those who spoke in God's name to kings and people, from Moses, Elijah and Isaiah to John the Baptist, and later movements that claimed the same gift."
---

The prophets of Israel called rulers and people back to the covenant and announced judgment and restoration. Their books make up a large part of the Old Testament, and the New Testament quotes them on almost every page. The thread continues with John the Baptist, the Revelation to John, and later movements in the church that claimed new prophecy.
