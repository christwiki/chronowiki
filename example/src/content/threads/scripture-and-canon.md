The Bible is a library formed over more than a thousand years. This thread traces which books were received as Scripture and when, the great translations that carried them into Greek, Latin, Gothic, Slavonic, German and English, and the discoveries of ancient manuscripts that show how the text was handed down. It also marks where the churches differ on the limits of the Old Testament.
