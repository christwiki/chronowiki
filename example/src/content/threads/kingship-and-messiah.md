---
title: "Kingship and Messiah"
order: 3
summary: "Israel's kings, the promise of a lasting throne for David, the loss of the monarchy, and the hope for an anointed deliverer that the New Testament applies to Jesus."
---

Messiah means anointed one, the title of Israel's kings. After Babylon ended the monarchy, the promise made to David became a hope for a future king. The Gospels tell the story of Jesus as the fulfilment of that hope, in a form that few expected: a king who is crucified.
