---
title: "Covenant and Promise"
order: 1
summary: "The binding promises between God and his people, from Noah and Abraham through Sinai and David to the new covenant proclaimed at the Last Supper."
---

Covenant is the Bible's own word for the relationship between God and his people. Each covenant builds on the last: a promise to all creation after the flood, a family chosen in Abraham, a nation formed at Sinai, a royal line promised to David, and a new covenant foretold by Jeremiah. The New Testament presents the death and resurrection of Jesus as that new covenant, which is why Christians call the two parts of their Bible the Old and New Testaments, that is, covenants.
