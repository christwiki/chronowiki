The world of the New Testament takes shape here. Alexander brings the Greek language, the Septuagint gives Scripture to the Greek-speaking world, and the Maccabean crisis leaves a lasting mark on Jewish hope. Pharisees, Sadducees and the community of the Dead Sea Scrolls emerge, and Rome becomes the ruling power.
