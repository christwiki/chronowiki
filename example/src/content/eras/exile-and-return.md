The exile is the great break in the Old Testament story. It is also one of the best documented periods: Babylonian chronicles, ration tablets and the Cyrus Cylinder stand beside Kings, Jeremiah, Ezekiel and Ezra. Out of the loss of king and temple came a faith centred on Scripture and a sharpened hope for restoration.
