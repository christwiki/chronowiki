summary: "The kingdom splits into Israel and Judah. Prophets confront kings, Assyria destroys the north, and for the first time inscriptions name the Bible's rulers."
---

From this era on, Assyrian and Babylonian records give fixed dates: Ahab at Qarqar in 853 BC, Jehu's tribute in 841 BC, the fall of Samaria in 722 BC, Sennacherib's campaign in 701 BC. The great prophets, Elijah, Amos, Hosea, Isaiah and Jeremiah, speak into these crises, and their words become Scripture.
