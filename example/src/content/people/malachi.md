The book's heading reads "Yahweh's word to Israel by Malachi" and gives no father's name, home or date [[bible:Mal 1:1]]. Malachi means "my messenger", and the same Hebrew word opens the promise "Behold, I send my messenger" [[bible:Mal 3:1]]. The ancient Greek translation took the heading that way, as the word of the Lord "by the hand of his messenger" [[cite:greek]], so it has long been asked whether Malachi is a personal name or a title.

His message belongs to Jerusalem after the temple was rebuilt; see [[event:malachi]]. The New Testament quotes his words about the messenger and about Elijah in connection with John the Baptist [[bible:Matt 11:10-14]] [[bible:Luke 1:17]].
