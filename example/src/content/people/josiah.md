Josiah became king at eight, after his father Amon was murdered, and reigned thirty-one years [[bible:2 Kings 21:23-24]] [[bible:2 Kings 22:1]]. Kings says that no king before or after him turned to the Lord as he did [[bible:2 Kings 23:25]]. For the finding of the book of the law and what followed see [[event:josiahs-reform]]. [[person:jeremiah]] began to prophesy in his thirteenth year [[bible:Jer 1:2]] and later held him up to his son as a king who did justice and judged the cause of the poor [[bible:Jer 22:15-16]].

He went out against Pharaoh [[person:necho-ii]], who was marching to the Euphrates, and met him at Megiddo. Kings says that Necho killed him there; Chronicles says that he was shot by archers and died after being brought back to Jerusalem [[bible:2 Kings 23:29-30]] [[bible:2 Chr 35:20-24]]. The Babylonian chronicle records an Egyptian army crossing the Euphrates in 609 BC [[cite:necho]], which gives the year. Kings tells of a man of God who foretold him by name in the days of [[person:jeroboam-i]] [[bible:1 Kings 13:2]], and Matthew's genealogy of Jesus includes him [[bible:Matt 1:10-11]].
