Simon, "who was called Thassi", was one of the five sons of [[person:mattathias]], who on his deathbed called him a man of counsel and told his brothers to listen to him [[bible:1 Macc 2:3, 65]]. He commanded in Galilee under [[person:judas-maccabeus]] [[bible:1 Macc 5:17-23]] and became leader when Jonathan was taken prisoner [[bible:1 Macc 13:1-9]].

Under him the Seleucid king gave up his claim to tribute, the foreign garrison left the citadel of [[place:jerusalem]], and an assembly of the people made him leader and high priest "until there should arise a faithful prophet" [[bible:1 Macc 13:36-42, 49-51]] [[bible:1 Macc 14:41]]. He built a monument over the family tomb at [[place:modein]] [[bible:1 Macc 13:27-30]]. He was murdered with two of his sons by his son-in-law Ptolemy in the 177th year of the Seleucid era [[bible:1 Macc 16:11-16]]. Josephus gives him a rule of eight years [[cite:reign]].
