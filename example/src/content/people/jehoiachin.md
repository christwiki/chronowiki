Jehoiachin came to the throne at eighteen, was besieged in Jerusalem by the Babylonian army, and gave himself up after a reign of three months [[bible:2 Kings 24:8-12]]. He was taken to [[place:babylon]] with his family and court [[bible:2 Kings 24:15]]. Babylonian ration lists record oil issued to the king of Judah and his retinue [[cite:rations]], and Kings ends with his release from prison in the thirty-seventh year of his exile [[bible:2 Kings 25:27-30]].

The exiles continued to count years by his captivity [[bible:Ezek 1:2]], and the line of David was traced through him: Zerubbabel, who led the return, was his grandson [[bible:1 Chr 3:17-19]], and Matthew's genealogy of Jesus passes through him [[bible:Matt 1:11-12]].
