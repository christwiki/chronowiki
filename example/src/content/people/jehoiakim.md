Eliakim son of [[person:josiah]] was made king by Pharaoh [[person:necho-ii]], who changed his name to Jehoiakim. He was twenty-five, reigned eleven years, and taxed the land to pay Egypt's tribute [[bible:2 Kings 23:34-36]]. After the Egyptian defeat at [[place:carchemish]] he served [[person:nebuchadnezzar-ii]] for three years and then rebelled [[bible:2 Kings 24:1]].

[[person:jeremiah]] condemned him for building his palace with unpaid labour and for shedding innocent blood [[bible:Jer 22:13-19]]. He had the prophet Uriah fetched back from Egypt and killed [[bible:Jer 26:20-23]], and when Jeremiah's scroll was read to him he cut it up and burned it piece by piece [[bible:Jer 36:20-26]]. Kings says only that he slept with his fathers [[bible:2 Kings 24:6]]. Chronicles says that Nebuchadnezzar bound him in fetters to take him to Babylon [[bible:2 Chr 36:6]]. His son [[person:jehoiachin]] followed him and gave up the city three months later; see [[event:first-deportation-to-babylon]].
