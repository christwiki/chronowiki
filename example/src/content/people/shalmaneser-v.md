Shalmaneser took the throne of Assyria and Babylon on the twenty-fifth of the month Tebetu, after the death of Tiglath-pileser, and died in the same month in his fifth year. The Babylonian chronicle records one act of his reign: he ravaged Samaria [[cite:chronicle]]. Kings says that he made [[person:hoshea-of-israel]] his vassal and that Assyria imprisoned Hoshea when he rebelled and laid siege to [[place:samaria]] [[bible:2 Kings 17:3-5]] [[bible:2 Kings 18:9]]. His successor [[person:sargon-ii]] claimed the capture of the city as his own. See [[event:fall-of-samaria]].
