---
name: Zerubbabel
role: Governor of Judah, descendant of David
summary: Grandson of King Jehoiachin and governor of Judah under Persia. He led exiles home and, with the priest Jeshua, began the rebuilding of the temple, which was finished in 515 BC.
---

Zerubbabel heads the list of those who returned from Babylon [[bible:Ezra 2:1-2]]. With [[person:jeshua-the-high-priest]] he rebuilt the altar and began the temple [[bible:Ezra 3:1-9]], and after a long halt resumed the work at the urging of the prophets Haggai and Zechariah [[bible:Ezra 5:1-2]].

As a descendant of David through [[person:jehoiachin]] [[bible:1 Chr 3:17-19]] he carried royal hopes: Haggai calls him the Lord's signet ring [[bible:Hag 2:20-23]]. Both Gospel genealogies of Jesus include him [[bible:Matt 1:12-13]] [[bible:Luke 3:27]].
