Alexander was the son of Philip of Macedon [[bible:1 Macc 1:1]]. Arrian, following Aristobulus, says that he died in the archon-year that corresponds to 323 BC, aged thirty-two years and eight months, after a reign of twelve years and eight months [[cite:death]]. In that time he defeated Darius III of Persia, took [[place:tyre]] [[cite:tyre]] and Gaza [[cite:gaza]], and founded [[place:alexandria]] in Egypt [[cite:egypt]].

1 Maccabees begins its history with him and says that his officers ruled after him, took crowns and "multiplied evils in the earth" [[bible:1 Macc 1:1-9]]. Daniel's visions speak of a king of Greece whose kingdom is broken and divided to the four winds [[bible:Dan 8:21-22]] [[bible:Dan 11:3-4]]. Josephus, more than four centuries later, tells of his visit to Jerusalem and his respect for the high priest [[cite:jaddua]]; see [[event:alexander-conquers-the-east]].
