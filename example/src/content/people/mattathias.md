Mattathias was a priest of the family of Joarib and had five sons: John, Simon, Judas, Eleazar and Jonathan [[bible:1 Macc 2:1-5]]. Josephus traces his line to an ancestor named Asamoneus [[cite:asamoneus]], from whom the dynasty of his descendants is called Hasmonean.

When the king's officers came to [[place:modein]] he refused to sacrifice, killed a Jew who did and the officer in charge, and fled to the hills [[bible:1 Macc 2:15-28]]. He decided that his followers would fight if attacked on the sabbath [[bible:1 Macc 2:39-41]]. He died in the 146th year of the Seleucid era, about 166 BC, leaving the command to his son [[person:judas-maccabeus]], and was buried at Modein [[bible:1 Macc 2:65-70]]. See [[event:maccabean-revolt]].
