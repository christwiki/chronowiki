summary: The high priest who returned from Babylon with Zerubbabel and rebuilt the altar and the temple with him. Zechariah's visions centre on him.
---

Jeshua, son of Jozadak, came back with the first returnees and rebuilt the altar in Jerusalem [[bible:Ezra 2:1-2; 3:1-2]]. Haggai and Zechariah, who call him Joshua, address him alongside [[person:zerubbabel]] [[bible:Hag 1:1]]. In Zechariah he is cleansed and reclothed in a vision and then crowned [[bible:Zech 3:1-10]] [[bible:Zech 6:9-13]].
