John was the son of [[person:simon-thassi]], who put him in command of the army [[bible:1 Macc 13:53]]. He escaped the plot in which his father was killed and succeeded him as high priest. 1 Maccabees ends by referring the reader to the chronicles of his high priesthood, which have not survived [[bible:1 Macc 16:19-24]].

The rest is known only from Josephus, writing some two centuries later. Early in his rule Antiochus VII besieged him in [[place:jerusalem]] and made terms [[cite:siege]]. Later he took Shechem and Mount Gerizim and subdued the Idumeans, who were allowed to remain if they were circumcised and kept the Jewish law [[cite:conquests]]. He left the Pharisees, urged on by a Sadducee friend, after a guest at his table challenged his right to the high priesthood [[cite:pharisees]]. Josephus gives him thirty-one years of rule and says that he was thought to have the gift of prophecy [[cite:death]]. See [[event:hasmonean-independence]].
