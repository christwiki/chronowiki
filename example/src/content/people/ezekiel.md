---
name: Ezekiel
role: Priest and prophet among the exiles in Babylonia
summary: A priest deported with Jehoiachin in 597 BC. His visions by the river Chebar speak of the glory of God leaving the temple, of dry bones restored to life and of a new temple.
---

Ezekiel was a priest, the son of Buzi, and was taken to Babylonia in the first deportation. His call came in the fifth year of that exile, by the river Chebar [[bible:Ezek 1:1-3]]. His book dates its oracles year by year from the deportation; the latest falls in the twenty-seventh year [[bible:Ezek 29:17]].

He announced the fall of Jerusalem before it happened [[bible:Ezek 4:1-3]] and the restoration of Israel afterwards, in the visions of the valley of dry bones [[bible:Ezek 37:1-14]] and of a rebuilt temple [[bible:Ezek 40:1-4]].
