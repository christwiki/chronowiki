---

Zedekiah was an uncle of [[person:jehoiachin]]. Nebuchadnezzar made him king in 597 BC and changed his name from Mattaniah [[bible:2 Kings 24:17-18]]. He rebelled against Babylon [[bible:2 Kings 24:20]]. During the siege that followed, [[person:jeremiah]] urged him to surrender, and he held back for fear of the Judeans who had already gone over to the enemy [[bible:Jer 38:14-23]].

When the city fell he fled, was captured near Jericho, saw his sons killed and was blinded and deported [[bible:2 Kings 25:4-7]].
