Cyrus, king of Anshan in Persia, overthrew Astyages, king of the Medes [[cite:medes]], and Croesus of Lydia [[cite:lydia]], and in 539 BC took [[place:babylon]], which his army entered without a battle [[cite:chronicle]]. In his own inscription he says that he restored gods and peoples to their homes [[cite:cylinder]]. Herodotus says that he fell in battle against the Massagetae after a reign of twenty-nine years [[cite:death]].

The Bible remembers him for the decree that ended the exile [[bible:Ezra 1:1-4]]. Isaiah 45 calls him the Lord's anointed [[bible:Isa 45:1]].
