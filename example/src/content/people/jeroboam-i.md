Jeroboam son of Nebat, of Zeredah in Ephraim, oversaw the forced labour of the house of Joseph under Solomon. The prophet Ahijah of Shiloh told him he would rule ten tribes; Solomon sought his life and he fled to Shishak in Egypt [[bible:1 Kings 11:26-40]]. After Solomon's death he returned, and the northern tribes made him king when they broke with Rehoboam [[bible:1 Kings 12:2-20]]; see the Kingdom Divides.

To keep his people from going to [[place:jerusalem]] he set up golden calves at [[place:bethel]] and Dan with a priesthood and a festival of his own [[bible:1 Kings 12:26-33]]. Ahijah then foretold the end of his house [[bible:1 Kings 14:7-16]]. He reigned twenty-two years [[bible:1 Kings 14:20]]; his son Nadab was killed within two years and the whole family with him [[bible:1 Kings 15:25-30]]. Kings judges later kings of Israel by whether they kept to "the sins of Jeroboam" [[bible:2 Kings 10:29]].
