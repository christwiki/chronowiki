---
name: Hoshea of Israel
altNames: [Hoshea son of Elah]
role: Last king of Israel, reigned nine years to c. 722 BC
summary: The last king of the northern kingdom. He seized the throne by killing Pekah, became a vassal of Assyria, withheld tribute and looked to Egypt, and was imprisoned before Samaria fell.
---

Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah, killed him and reigned nine years in [[place:samaria]] [[bible:2 Kings 15:30]] [[bible:2 Kings 17:1]]. Kings says that he did evil, though not like the kings before him [[bible:2 Kings 17:2]]. He paid tribute to [[person:shalmaneser-v]], then withheld it and sent envoys to the king of Egypt. The Assyrian king put him in prison and besieged his capital [[bible:2 Kings 17:3-5]]. Kings does not say what became of him. See [[event:fall-of-samaria]].
