Nehemiah son of Hacaliah served King [[person:artaxerxes-i]] as cupbearer at [[place:susa]] [[bible:Neh 1:1, 11]]. He was governor of Judah from the king's twentieth year to the thirty-second, and came back for a second term after a visit to court [[bible:Neh 5:14]] [[bible:Neh 13:6-7]]. He rebuilt the wall of [[place:jerusalem]] in the face of [[person:sanballat]] and his allies [[bible:Neh 6:15-16]], and his account is punctuated by short prayers that God would remember what he had done [[bible:Neh 5:19; 13:14, 31]].

Ben Sira honours him among the great men of Israel [[bible:Sir 49:13]], and a letter preserved in 2 Maccabees credits him with founding a library of the books about the kings and prophets and the writings of David [[bible:2 Macc 2:13]]. Josephus says that he lived to a great age [[cite:josephus]].
