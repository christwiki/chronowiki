---
name: Mordecai
role: Jewish official at Susa in the book of Esther
summary: Esther's cousin and guardian, a Jew of the tribe of Benjamin at the Persian court. In the book of Esther his refusal to bow to Haman sets off the plot against the Jews, and he ends as the king's chief minister.
---

The book of Esther introduces Mordecai as a Benjaminite living in the citadel of [[place:susa]], from a family deported with King Jeconiah, who had brought up his orphaned cousin [[person:esther]] [[bible:Esth 2:5-7]]. He sat at the king's gate, uncovered a plot against the king's life, and refused to bow to Haman [[bible:Esth 2:21-23]] [[bible:Esth 3:2-6]]. After Haman's fall the king gave Mordecai his signet ring, and Mordecai wrote the decree that saved the Jews and the letters that established Purim [[bible:Esth 8:2, 9-10]] [[bible:Esth 9:20-22]]. The book ends with him next in rank to the king [[bible:Esth 10:3]].

Outside the book, 2 Maccabees calls the feast "the day of Mordecai" [[bible:2 Macc 15:36]].
