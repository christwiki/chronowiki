The book of Nehemiah calls him Sanballat the Horonite and never gives him a title. He was displeased when Nehemiah arrived, mocked the building of the wall before "the army of Samaria", plotted to attack the builders and tried to lure Nehemiah away or frighten him into hiding [[bible:Neh 2:10]] [[bible:Neh 4:1-8]] [[bible:Neh 6:1-14]]. A grandson of the high priest Eliashib married his daughter, and Nehemiah drove the man out [[bible:Neh 13:28]].

His office is known from a letter written by the Jews of Elephantine in 407 BC, which says that they had sent their appeal to Delaiah and Shelemiah, the sons of Sanballat, governor of [[place:samaria]] [[cite:elephantine]].

Josephus has a Sanballat sent to Samaria by the last Darius, a century later, who marries his daughter to the brother of a high priest and promises him a temple on Mount Gerizim [[cite:josephus]]. How that account relates to Nehemiah's is not settled.
