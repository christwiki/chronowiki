---
name: Daniel
altNames: [Belteshazzar]
role: Judean exile and seer at the court of Babylon
summary: A Judean taken to Babylon as a youth who, according to the book that bears his name, interpreted dreams for its kings, survived the lions' den and saw visions of the kingdoms to come.
---

The book of Daniel presents him as one of the young nobles of Judah brought to [[place:babylon]] under [[person:nebuchadnezzar-ii]], trained for the court and renamed Belteshazzar [[bible:Dan 1:3-7]]. He interprets the dreams of Nebuchadnezzar and the writing on the wall for [[person:belshazzar]], and is thrown to the lions for praying to his God [[bible:Dan 2:26-28]] [[bible:Dan 5:13-17]] [[bible:Dan 6:10-23]]. The book says that he was still active in the third year of Cyrus [[bible:Dan 10:1]].

Later writers hold him up as an example. 1 Maccabees recalls his rescue from the lions [[bible:1 Macc 2:60]], and in Matthew Jesus calls him "Daniel the prophet" [[bible:Matt 24:15]]. When the book was written, and how far it records history, are debated; see [[event:daniel-in-babylon]].
