---
name: Hilkiah
role: High priest in Jerusalem under Josiah
summary: High priest in the reign of Josiah. He found the book of the law during repairs to the temple and led the delegation that consulted the prophetess Huldah.
---

Hilkiah was high priest when [[person:josiah]] ordered the temple repaired. He told the king's secretary Shaphan that he had found the book of the law in the house of the Lord, and handed it to him [[bible:2 Kings 22:4-8]]. He led the delegation that the king sent to the prophetess [[person:huldah]] [[bible:2 Kings 22:12-14]], and he was ordered to clear the temple of the vessels made for Baal and Asherah [[bible:2 Kings 23:4]]. The genealogies make him an ancestor of Jehozadak, who went into exile, and of Ezra [[bible:1 Chr 6:13-15]] [[bible:Ezra 7:1-2]].

The father of [[person:jeremiah]] was also a priest named Hilkiah, from Anathoth [[bible:Jer 1:1]]. The texts do not connect the two. See [[event:josiahs-reform]].
