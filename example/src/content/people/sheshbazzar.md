---
name: Sheshbazzar
role: Prince of Judah and governor at the first return
summary: The leader to whom Cyrus entrusted the temple vessels in 538 BC. He brought them to Jerusalem and is credited with laying the temple's foundations.
---

Ezra calls Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah and says that Cyrus's treasurer counted out the temple vessels to him [[bible:Ezra 1:7-11]]. A later report to King Darius names him governor and says that he laid the foundations of the temple [[bible:Ezra 5:14-16]]. Nothing more is known of him, and how he relates to [[person:zerubbabel]], who leads the work in the rest of Ezra, is not explained in the text.
