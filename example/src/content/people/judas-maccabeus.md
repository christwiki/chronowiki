Judas, "who was called Maccabaeus", took command when his father [[person:mattathias]] died [[bible:1 Macc 2:4, 66]] [[bible:1 Macc 3:1]]. With a small force he defeated one Seleucid army after another [[bible:1 Macc 3:10-24]] [[bible:1 Macc 4:1-35]], then cleansed the temple and dedicated a new altar on the twenty-fifth of Chislev [[bible:1 Macc 4:36-59]]. See [[event:maccabean-revolt]].

The war continued after the rededication. Judas defeated the general Nicanor, a victory kept as a yearly festival on the thirteenth of Adar [[bible:1 Macc 7:39-49]], and sent envoys to Rome, where the senate granted a treaty of friendship [[bible:1 Macc 8:17-30]]. He was killed fighting a much larger army in the 152nd year of the Seleucid era, about 160 BC, and was buried at [[place:modein]] [[bible:1 Macc 9:3-19]]. His brother Jonathan succeeded him [[bible:1 Macc 9:28-31]].
