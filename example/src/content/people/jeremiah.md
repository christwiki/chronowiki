---
name: Jeremiah
role: Prophet in Jerusalem, active c. 627–after 586 BC
summary: Prophet of Judah's last decades. He warned that Babylon would destroy Jerusalem, was imprisoned for it, lived through the city's fall, and promised a new covenant written on the heart.
---

Jeremiah came from a priestly family at Anathoth and was called in the thirteenth year of King Josiah [[bible:Jer 1:1-10]]. For forty years he warned that Judah's unfaithfulness would bring conquest from the north [[bible:Jer 1:13-16]], and he urged King Zedekiah to submit to Babylon [[bible:Jer 27:12-13]]. He was beaten, tried and imprisoned for it [[bible:Jer 26:7-16]] [[bible:Jer 37:11-21]].

He wrote to the exiles of 597 BC, telling them to settle and to expect a return after seventy years [[bible:Jer 29:1-14]]. After the city fell he was taken to Egypt by a group he had warned not to go there [[bible:Jer 42:19; 43:4-7]]. His promise of a new covenant [[bible:Jer 31:31-34]] is quoted at length in the New Testament [[bible:Heb 8:6-13]].
