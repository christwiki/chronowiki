Sargon took the throne of Assyria on the twelfth of the month Tebetu in the winter of 722/721 BC [[cite:accession]]. In the inscription of his palace at Khorsabad he says that he besieged and conquered [[place:samaria]], deported 27,290 people and imposed tribute on those who remained [[cite:display]]. A prism from Nimrud adds that he resettled the city with people from other lands he had conquered [[cite:prism]]. The Bible names him once, when his commander took Ashdod [[bible:Isa 20:1]]. See [[event:fall-of-samaria]].
