---
name: Zechariah
altNames: [Zechariah son of Berechiah]
role: Prophet in Jerusalem, 520–518 BC
summary: A prophet of the return from exile who, with Haggai, urged the rebuilding of the temple. His book of night visions and later oracles is quoted in the Gospel accounts of Jesus's entry into Jerusalem and his death.
---

Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, began to prophesy in the eighth month of the second year of [[person:darius-i]], two months after [[person:haggai]], and his last dated message falls in the fourth year, 518 BC [[bible:Zech 1:1]] [[bible:Zech 7:1]]. Ezra calls him the son of Iddo and credits the two prophets with getting the temple finished [[bible:Ezra 5:1; 6:14]]. His visions promise that God has returned to Jerusalem, show the high priest [[person:jeshua-the-high-priest]] cleansed and crowned, and assure [[person:zerubbabel]] that he will complete the building [[bible:Zech 1:16]] [[bible:Zech 3:1-5]] [[bible:Zech 4:6-10]] [[bible:Zech 6:9-13]].

Chapters 9–14 carry no dates. The Gospels draw on them: the king who comes riding on a donkey [[bible:Zech 9:9]] [[bible:Matt 21:4-5]], the shepherd who is struck [[bible:Zech 13:7]] [[bible:Matt 26:31]] and the one "whom they have pierced" [[bible:Zech 12:10]] [[bible:John 19:37]].
