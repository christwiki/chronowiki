Jedaniah is known from the Aramaic papyri found on [[place:elephantine]]. A letter of the fifth year of Darius II, from one Hananiah, is addressed to Jedaniah and his colleagues, "the Jewish garrison" [[cite:festival]]. In 407 BC the petition to Bagohi, governor of Judah, is sent in the name of Jedaniah and his colleagues, the priests at Elephantine, and of all the Jews of the place [[cite:petition]].

A Jedaniah son of Gemariah stands first among five men who offer a payment if the temple is rebuilt and only incense and meal-offering are offered in it [[cite:offer]]. Cowley, who edited the papyri in 1923, took him for the same man (note on no. 33, line 1). See [[event:elephantine-temple|the temple at Elephantine]].
