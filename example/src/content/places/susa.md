Susa lay in Elam, east of the Tigris, at modern Shush in south-western Iran. The Persian kings kept a residence there: Herodotus has Xerxes return to Susa after his campaign in Greece [[cite:herodotus]]. The Bible calls it Shushan. Daniel sees a vision "in the citadel of Susa, which is in the province of Elam" [[bible:Dan 8:2]], the story of [[person:esther]] takes place in its palace [[bible:Esth 1:2]], and [[person:nehemiah]] was serving the king there when news reached him of the state of Jerusalem [[bible:Neh 1:1-3]].
