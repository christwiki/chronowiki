Omri bought the hill from Shemer for two talents of silver, built a city on it and named it Samaria [[bible:1 Kings 16:24]]. It remained the capital of Israel's kings. Ahab built a temple of Baal there, which Jehu destroyed [[bible:1 Kings 16:32; 2 Kings 10:25-27]], and the prophets Amos and Isaiah speak of it as the head of the northern kingdom [[bible:Amos 6:1; Isa 7:9]]. The king of Assyria took the city after a three-year siege, deported its people and settled others in their place [[bible:2 Kings 17:5-6, 24]]; see [[event:fall-of-samaria]].

In the New Testament the name is used for the region between Judaea and Galilee, through which Jesus travelled [[bible:John 4:3-9]] and where Philip preached [[bible:Acts 8:5]].
