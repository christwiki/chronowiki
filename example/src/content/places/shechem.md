Shechem stood in the pass between Mount Ebal and Mount Gerizim; its mound, Tell Balata, is on the eastern edge of modern Nablus. It is Abram's first stopping place in Canaan, where he builds an altar [[bible:Gen 12:6-7]]. Jacob buys a piece of ground outside the city and sets up an altar there [[bible:Gen 33:18-20]], and the bones of Joseph are later buried in that plot [[bible:Josh 24:32]]. Joshua gathers the tribes at Shechem to renew the covenant [[bible:Josh 24:1]] [[bible:Josh 24:25]]. Rehoboam comes here to be made king, the northern tribes break away, and [[person:jeroboam-i|Jeroboam]] makes it his first residence [[bible:1 Kgs 12:1]] [[bible:1 Kgs 12:16-25]]. In John's Gospel [[person:jesus]] speaks with a Samaritan woman at Jacob's well, near the ground Jacob gave to Joseph [[bible:John 4:5-6]].
