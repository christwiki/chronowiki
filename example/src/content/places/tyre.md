Tyre was a city of the Phoenician coast, built on an island close to the shore. Its king Hiram of Tyre supplied cedar and cypress timber for the temple of Solomon [[bible:1 Kings 5:1-12]], and Ezekiel describes its trade and foretells its fall [[bible:Ezek 26:1-14]] [[bible:Ezek 27:1-9]]. [[person:alexander-the-great]] took it in 332 BC by building a mole from the mainland to the island [[cite:mole]]. Jesus went into its district [[bible:Mark 7:24-31]], and Paul stayed a week with the disciples there on his way to Jerusalem [[bible:Acts 21:3-7]].
