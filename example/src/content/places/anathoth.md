Anathoth was one of the towns assigned to the priests in the territory of Benjamin [[bible:Josh 21:17-19]]. Solomon sent the priest Abiathar away to his fields there [[bible:1 Kings 2:26]]. [[person:jeremiah]] came from its priestly families [[bible:Jer 1:1]]. Men of the town threatened his life [[bible:Jer 11:21-23]], and during the last siege of [[place:jerusalem]] he bought a field there from his cousin as a sign that land would be bought and sold again [[bible:Jer 32:6-15]]. Isaiah names it on the path of an army advancing on Jerusalem from the north [[bible:Isa 10:28-32]], and 128 of its men are listed among those who returned from exile [[bible:Ezra 2:23]]. The name survives in the village of Anata, north-east of the Old City, and the map places the ancient town there.
