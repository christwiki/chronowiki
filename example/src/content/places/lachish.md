Lachish lay in the lowlands of Judah, about 45 kilometres south-west of [[place:jerusalem]]. The book of Joshua tells of its capture [[bible:Josh 10:31-32]], Chronicles lists it among the towns Rehoboam fortified [[bible:2 Chr 11:5-9]], and King Amaziah was killed there [[bible:2 Kings 14:19]]. In 701 BC [[person:sennacherib]] was encamped at Lachish when [[person:hezekiah]] sent his submission, and from there he sent his officers to Jerusalem [[bible:2 Kings 18:14]] [[bible:2 Kings 18:17]]. The reliefs from Sennacherib's palace show the city's fall, with a caption that names it [[cite:relief]]. When the Babylonians invaded a century later, Lachish and Azekah were the last fortified towns of Judah still standing besides Jerusalem [[bible:Jer 34:7]].
