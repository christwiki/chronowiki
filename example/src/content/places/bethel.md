Bethel is identified with the village of Beitin, about seventeen kilometres north of [[place:jerusalem]]. Genesis says that its older name was Luz [[bible:Gen 28:19]]. Abram built an altar in the hills between Bethel and Ai [[bible:Gen 12:8]]. Jacob dreamed here on his way to Haran and set up a stone as a pillar, and on his return he built an altar [[bible:Gen 28:10-22]] [[bible:Gen 35:6-7]]. In the time of the judges Israel went up to Bethel to ask counsel of God [[bible:Judg 20:18]], and Samuel came here on his yearly circuit [[bible:1 Sam 7:16]]. When the kingdom divided, [[person:jeroboam-i|Jeroboam]] set up a golden calf at Bethel as a rival to the temple in Jerusalem [[bible:1 Kgs 12:26-29]]. Amos was told not to prophesy there, "for it is the king's sanctuary" [[bible:Amos 7:13]], and King [[person:josiah]] later destroyed its altar [[bible:2 Kgs 23:15]].
