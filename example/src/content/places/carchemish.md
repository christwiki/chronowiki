Carchemish stood on the bank of the upper Euphrates; its ruins lie on the present border between Turkey and Syria. Isaiah lists it among the cities that had fallen to Assyria [[bible:Isa 10:9]]. Chronicles says that Pharaoh [[person:necho-ii]] was marching to fight at Carchemish when Josiah met him at Megiddo [[bible:2 Chr 35:20-22]]. Four years later an Egyptian army lay in the city when the Babylonian crown prince [[person:nebuchadnezzar-ii]] crossed the river and defeated it [[cite:chronicle]] [[bible:Jer 46:2]]. See [[event:battle-of-carchemish]].
