Jerusalem stands in the Judean hills, about 750 metres above sea level. David took it from the Jebusites and made it his capital [[bible:2 Sam 5:6-10]], and the temple built there made it the centre of Israel's worship. It was destroyed by Babylon in 587/586 BC and by Rome in AD 70, and each time became the focus of hope for restoration.
