Modein was the home of [[person:mattathias]] and the place where he refused the king's sacrifice [[bible:1 Macc 2:1, 15-28]]. He and his sons were buried there [[bible:1 Macc 2:70]] [[bible:1 Macc 9:19]], and [[person:simon-thassi]] raised over the family tomb a monument with seven pyramids that could be seen from the sea [[bible:1 Macc 13:25-30]]. The village lay in the foothills between Jerusalem and the coast, in the area of the modern city of Modi'in. Its exact site has not been settled; the coordinates are those given by the Pleiades gazetteer.
