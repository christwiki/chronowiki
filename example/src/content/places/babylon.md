Babylon lay on the Euphrates, some 85 kilometres south of modern Baghdad. Under [[person:nebuchadnezzar-ii]] it was the capital of the empire that conquered Judah. Genesis sets the tower of Babel here [[bible:Gen 11:1-9]], the exiles of Judah were brought to it [[bible:2 Kings 24:15-16]], and [[person:cyrus-ii]] took it in 539 BC. The New Testament gives its name to another city, which most interpreters take to be Rome [[bible:1 Pet 5:13]] [[bible:Rev 17:5]].
