Elephantine is an island in the Nile at Aswan, just below the First Cataract. The Aramaic papyri found there call it Yeb, "the fortress", and name Syene, the modern Aswan on the east bank, as a second fortress. A letter of 407 BC from the Judeans who lived on the island says that their temple of YHW stood there before Cambyses came to Egypt, and that the priests of Khnum and the chief official of the place had it destroyed in the fourteenth year of Darius [[cite:petition]]; see [[event:elephantine-temple|the temple at Elephantine]]. The papyri cited in this wiki came from excavations on the island directed by Otto Rubensohn and Friedrich Zucker and are kept in Berlin and Cairo. Ezekiel names Syene in marking the extent of Egypt [[bible:Ezek 29:10]].
