Rome was the seat of the senate that named [[person:herod-the-great]] king of the Jews [[cite:herod]] and of the emperors from Augustus onwards. Visitors from Rome were in Jerusalem at Pentecost [[bible:Acts 2:10]]. Paul wrote a letter to the Christians of the city before he had seen it [[bible:Rom 1:7-13]], and Acts ends with his arrival there as a prisoner [[bible:Acts 28:14-16]]. A greeting at the end of 1 Peter comes from "Babylon", usually understood as a name for Rome [[bible:1 Pet 5:13]].
