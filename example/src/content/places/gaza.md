Gaza stood near the Mediterranean coast at the southern end of the land, on the road to Egypt. In the Old Testament it is one of the five cities of the Philistines [[bible:1 Sam 6:17]]: Samson carries off its gates, and is later brought there blinded and set to grind in the prison [[bible:Judg 16:1-3, 21]]. In Acts an angel sends Philip the Evangelist to "the way that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza", a desert road, where he meets the Ethiopian official [[bible:Acts 8:26]]. See the Gospel Reaches Samaria and an Ethiopian Official.
