Nippur lay in central Babylonia, south-east of [[place:babylon]]; its ruins are at Nuffar in Iraq. The Bible does not name the city. Ezekiel places himself and his fellow exiles "by the river Chebar", at a settlement called Tel-abib [[bible:Ezek 1:1-3]] [[bible:Ezek 3:15]]. A business archive of the fifth century BC, excavated at Nippur in 1893, records a delivery to be made at a canal called Kabaru [[cite:murashu]]. Its first editor identified the canal with the Chebar and noted that many of the people named in the archive have Judean names [[cite:murashu-editor]]. Where Tel-abib itself lay is not known; the timeline places [[person:ezekiel]]'s visions at Nippur as the nearest fixed point.
