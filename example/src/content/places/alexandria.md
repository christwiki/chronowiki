[[person:alexander-the-great]] marked out the city himself, beside Lake Mareotis on the coast west of the Nile delta [[cite:founding]]. It became the seat of the Ptolemies, the Greek kings of Egypt. Josephus says that the first Ptolemy settled Jews there with the same civic rights as the Macedonians [[cite:jews]], and the Letter of Aristeas sets the translation of the law of Moses into Greek on the island of Pharos in its harbour [[cite:pharos]]. Apollos, "an Alexandrian by race", came from its Jewish community [[bible:Acts 18:24]].
