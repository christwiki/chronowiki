Haran lies in the plain of Upper Mesopotamia, south-east of modern Şanlıurfa and close to the Syrian border. The Romans knew it as Carrhae. Terah settled and died here [[bible:Gen 11:31-32]], and from here Abram set out for Canaan [[bible:Gen 12:4-5]]. His brother's family stayed in the region: Rebekah sends Jacob to her brother Laban in Haran [[bible:Gen 27:43]], and Jacob serves Laban there for twenty years [[bible:Gen 29:4-5]] [[bible:Gen 31:41]]. Later books name Haran among the cities taken by Assyria [[bible:2 Kgs 19:12]] and among the trading partners of [[place:tyre]] [[bible:Ezek 27:23]].
