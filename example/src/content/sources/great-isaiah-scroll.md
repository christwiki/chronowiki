The scroll is one of the seven found in the first cave at Qumran in 1947. It is 7.34 metres long and almost complete. The Israel Museum dates it to about 125 BC, roughly a thousand years before the oldest copies of the Hebrew Bible known until then.

Its text is close to the traditional Hebrew text, with many differences of spelling and wording. It shows that by the late second century BC the sixty-six chapters of Isaiah were copied as a single book. It cannot show how or when the book was put together, which is the question debated for chapters 40–55; see [[event:prophecies-of-return]].
