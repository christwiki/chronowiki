Clay cylinders bearing this text were found at Ur, where [[person:nabonidus]] restored the temple tower of the moon god Sin. The inscription describes the work and closes with a prayer that the god keep the king from sin and put reverence into the heart of "Belshazzar, the eldest son".

It is a contemporary document from Belshazzar's own lifetime. It shows that the [[person:belshazzar]] of Daniel 5 was a real person and the eldest son of Nabonidus. It does not call him king.
