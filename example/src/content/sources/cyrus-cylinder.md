The cylinder is a foundation deposit of the kind Mesopotamian kings buried in the buildings they restored. It was excavated at Babylon in 1879. In the manner of such royal inscriptions it denounces the previous king, Nabonidus, says that the god Marduk chose [[person:cyrus-ii]] to replace him, and reports that Cyrus entered Babylon without a battle.

Its closing section says that Cyrus sent the images of gods back to their sanctuaries in a list of cities beyond the Tigris and returned their people to their settlements. Judah and Jerusalem are not named. The text matters for [[event:edict-of-cyrus]] because it records, in the king's own words, measures of the same kind as the decree in Ezra.
