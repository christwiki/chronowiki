The Babylonian chronicles are terse year-by-year records of the king's campaigns, written in Akkadian on clay tablets. This tablet, known as Chronicle 5 in A. K. Grayson's edition (ABC 5), runs from the last year of Nabopolassar to the eleventh year of [[person:nebuchadnezzar-ii]].

Its entry for the seventh year reports the siege of "the city of Judah", its capture on the second day of the month Addaru, the removal of its king and the appointment of another. It is the only Babylonian account of [[event:first-deportation-to-babylon]], and it supplies the exact date. The tablet breaks off before the destruction of Jerusalem a decade later, for which no Babylonian chronicle survives.
